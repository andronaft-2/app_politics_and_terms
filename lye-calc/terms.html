3. Lye safety and the calculations

  • Sodium and potassium hydroxide are caustic and can cause severe burns and eye damage. Follow the safety instructions of your lye supplier: wear eye protection and gloves, work with ventilation, add lye to water (never water to lye), and keep children and pets away.
  • The app does arithmetic from published saponification (SAP) values and fatty acid profiles, mainly the SoapCalc oil list; the sources are listed in the app under Settings → Safety & sources. Real oils vary by batch and supplier, and scales and lye purity vary too. The results depend on the oils, amounts and settings you enter.
  • Check every new recipe with a second lye calculator or a reliable source before you weigh lye, and make a test batch. You are solely responsible for your recipes, how you make soap and the safety of anything you make, use, give away or sell.
  • Soap qualities, warnings and starter recipes are general guidance, not professional, chemical or medical advice.
