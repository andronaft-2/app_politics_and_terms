PoolPro privacy policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Operator: Dre Dev

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

PoolPro: Chemistry & Route is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It calculates pool chemical doses and keeps a log of your water tests. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run accounts or ads, and we don't sell or share your data. The app sends anonymous usage events so we can see which features help (see Usage analytics).

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • your pools: name, volume, surface, chlorine source, targets and chosen products;
  • water tests, the chemicals you added and your notes;
  • for service technicians: client names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, service days and the monthly fee you type in;
  • your business profile printed on client reports: name, phone, email and note;
  • salt cell output and settings, the date a chlorine jug was bought, and ppm-per-drop values from your test kit, if you enter them;
  • settings such as units and theme.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete a pool or test, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

Once a week the app also saves a copy of this data as a file on your phone, so it can be restored if something goes wrong. The last four copies are kept. On iPhone they are visible in the Files app (On My iPhone › PoolPro); on Android they stay in the app's own storage. Like the rest of the app's data, they are included in your device backup if it is turned on, and are never sent to us.

Files you share or open

  • Backups, CSV exports, client reports and shared results: the app creates the file or text on your device and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes, and we never receive a copy.
  • Restore from backup: the app reads only the file you pick.
  • Directions: when you tap Directions on a route stop, the client's address is opened in Google Maps (or your browser), which then handles it under Google's privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Usage analytics (PostHog)

In app versions released after 27 November 2026, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, Inc., a product analytics service; the data is stored in the EU (eu.i.posthog.com). The events show which screens and features are used, the onboarding steps, when the Pro screen is shown, when a purchase is started, completes or fails, when a PDF is exported, and when the app is installed, opened or moved to the background. Each event also carries the device model, operating system and app version. IP addresses are not stored.

The events contain no names, email addresses or anything you type (pool names, test readings, notes or client details). There are no session recordings, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. The events are tied only to a random ID created by the app, not to who you are, and there is no account.

We use them to understand which features help and to fix the places where people get stuck on the way to a result or to Pro. The events are kept as long as needed for product analytics and then deleted. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: only to verify purchases, to send the usage events above and to open links you tap.
  • Notifications: only for test reminders you turn on and for the timer between products in step-by-step dosing. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.
  • The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

PoolPro is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, export, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. If you keep client details for your business, you are responsible for handling them lawfully. For questions about subscription data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

PoolPro is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.

Як і решта даних застосунку, вони потрапляють у резервну копію пристрою, якщо її ввімкнено, і ніколи не надсилаються нам.

У версіях застосунку, випущених після 27 листопада 2026 року, застосунок надсилає анонімні події використання сервісу продуктової аналітики PostHog, Inc.; дані зберігаються в ЄС (eu.i.posthog.com). Події показують, якими екранами й функціями користуються, кроки знайомства із застосунком, коли показано екран Pro, коли покупку розпочато, завершено або вона не вдалася, коли експортовано PDF, а також коли застосунок встановлено, відкрито чи згорнуто. У подіях немає імен, адрес email і нічого з того, що ви вводите (назв басейнів, результатів аналізів, нотаток чи даних клієнтів). Немає записів сеансів, реклами й стеження в інших застосунках чи на сайтах. Події пов'язані лише з випадковим ідентифікатором, який створює застосунок, а не з вашою особою; облікового запису немає.

Вони потрібні, щоб розуміти, які функції допомагають, і виправляти місця, де люди зупиняються на шляху до результату чи Pro. Події зберігаються стільки, скільки потрібно для аналітики використання, а потім видаляються. Див. політику конфіденційності PostHog.
