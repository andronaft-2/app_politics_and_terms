PoolPro privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Operator: Dre Dev

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

PoolPro: Chemistry & Route is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It calculates pool chemical doses and keeps a log of your water tests. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • your pools: name, volume, surface, chlorine source, targets and chosen products;
  • water tests, the chemicals you added and your notes;
  • for service technicians: client names, addresses, phone numbers, emails and service days you type in;
  • your business profile printed on client reports: name, phone, email and note;
  • settings such as units and theme.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete a pool or test, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

Files you share or open

  • Backups, CSV exports and client reports: the app creates the file on your device and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes, and we never receive a copy.
  • Restore from backup: the app reads only the file you pick.
  • Directions: when you tap Directions on a route stop, the client's address is opened in Google Maps (or your browser), which then handles it under Google's privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: only to verify subscriptions and to open links you tap.
  • The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

PoolPro is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, export, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. If you keep client details for your business, you are responsible for handling them lawfully. For questions about subscription data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

PoolPro is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.

Дані на вашому пристрої

Застосунок зберігає лише в пам'яті застосунку на вашому телефоні:

  • для сервісних техніків: імена клієнтів, адреси, телефони, email і дні обслуговування, які ви вводите;
  • профіль вашої фірми для звітів клієнтам: назва, телефон, email і примітка;

Ці дані не надсилаються ні нам, ні комусь іншому. Вони видаляються, коли ви видаляєте басейн чи аналіз, очищаєте дані застосунку або видаляєте його. Вони можуть потрапити в резервну копію пристрою (iCloud або Google), на яку поширюються правила Apple і Google.

Файли, якими ви ділитеся чи які відкриваєте

  • Відновлення з резервної копії: застосунок читає лише файл, який ви обрали.
  • Маршрут: коли ви натискаєте Directions біля зупинки, адреса клієнта відкривається в Google Maps (або браузері), і далі на неї поширюється політика конфіденційності Google.
