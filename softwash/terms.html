Real SH strength drops with age and heat, injectors draw differently from their rating, and surfaces react differently.

SH is corrosive. Always follow the label and safety data sheet (SDS) of every product, the cleaning instructions of the surface's manufacturer (for example roofing and siding warranties), and local rules on chemicals and runoff.
