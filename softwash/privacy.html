SoftWash Pro privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Operator: Dre Dev

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

SoftWash Pro: Mix & Quote is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It is a mixing calculator and quoting tool for soft wash and pressure washing businesses. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • calculator inputs, saved quotes and jobs, including client names and job addresses you type;
  • your business profile shown on PDF quotes: company name, phone, email, terms note and deposit;
  • your price book (prices per surface, mix strengths, chemical prices) and settings such as units, currency, theme and the count of free quotes used.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete a job, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

Quotes, load lists and backups you share

When you tap "Send quote", the app creates a PDF on your device and opens your phone's share menu. A copied load list goes to your clipboard. "Back up jobs" and "Export jobs (CSV)" create a file on your device and open the share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it, and we never receive a copy.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: only to verify subscriptions and to open links you tap (these terms, the privacy policy, sources).
  • The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos, and sends no notifications.

Children

SoftWash Pro is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change, export or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. For questions about subscription data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

SoftWash Pro is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.
