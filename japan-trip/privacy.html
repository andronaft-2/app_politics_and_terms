Japan Trip Planner privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

Japan Trip Planner is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It helps you plan a trip to Japan. Everything you enter stays on your device. We run no accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

  • your trips (the current one and any you keep in the archive): dates, cities, travellers, budget, day plan, bookings and booking codes, stays and addresses, expenses, packing ticks and notes;
  • the passport country you choose, used only to show the visa rule for your trip;
  • your info card in the Emergency screen (name, blood type, allergies, medicines, contacts, insurance), your embassy phone and a currency rate you type;
  • files you attach to a booking (Pro): the app copies the file you pick into its own storage on your phone;
  • settings such as language and reminders.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the trip, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be part of your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

Sharing and export

If you export a PDF or a trip file, the app creates it on your device and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or person to share with. If you open a trip file, the app reads only the file you pick.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Listening to phrases

The "Listen" button reads a Japanese phrase with your phone's own text-to-speech service. The app sends the phrase only to that service on your phone, never to us. If your phone has no Japanese voice yet, its speech service (for example Google's) may download one or use its online voice the first time; that is covered by the speech provider's policy.

Links to other sites

Buttons for maps, official pages and phone calls open your maps app, browser or dialler. Those apps and sites have their own policies. The app has no live weather, maps or rates and makes no other network requests of its own.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for deadline reminders and a morning note on trip days. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.
  • Alarms and reminders (Android): so a reminder for a ticket sale arrives at the exact minute. You can turn it off in system settings; reminders then may come later.
  • Files: only a file you pick (to attach to a booking or to open a trip file) is read.
  • Internet: only to check subscriptions and to open links you tap.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. For questions about subscription data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Information in the app

The app shows rules, prices and dates with the day they were checked. They are for planning, can be out of date and are not medical or legal advice. See the terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Japan Trip Planner is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.
