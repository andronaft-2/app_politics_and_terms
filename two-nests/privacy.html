Two Nests privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

Two Nests: Spot the Difference is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It is a spot-the-difference game suitable for children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included. It has no accounts, no ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no advertising ID, no tracking, no chat and no user content that leaves the device. We don't sell or share any data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only in its own storage on the phone:

  • game progress: which rounds were finished, differences found in an unfinished round, stickers earned and where they were placed, days of the daily round;
  • settings: sounds, vibration, reduce motion, difficulty, language, and whether the Full game was bought.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

The Full game purchase

The Full game is a one-time purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see payment details. It can only be started by an adult, behind a parental check. To know whether the Full game is owned, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID (a random number, not linked to a name, email or advertising ID), the purchase status and basic technical information that any internet request carries, such as the OS version and IP address. The app sends RevenueCat no other attributes. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Progress export and import

In the parents' corner (behind the parental check), "Save progress to a file" creates a file with the progress and opens the phone's share menu, so you can keep it where you choose. "Load progress from a file" reads only the file you pick. Nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no notifications, location, camera, photos, microphone or contacts. It uses the internet only to check the Full game purchase.

Children

Two Nests is made to be safe for children. A child's path through the game never leaves the app and never shows a price: the purchase, restore, ratings, links out and settings sit behind a parental check. The app does not collect personal information from children (or anyone else), so there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side; uninstalling the app deletes everything on the device.

Your rights

Because the data stays on your device, you can see, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling it. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Two Nests is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.

Покупка «Повна гра»

Повна гра — разова покупка, яку обробляють Apple App Store або Google Play; платіжних даних ми не бачимо. Почати покупку може лише дорослий, після перевірки для батьків. Щоб знати, чи куплено повну гру, застосунок використовує RevenueCat, який отримує анонімний ідентифікатор користувача застосунку (випадкове число, не пов'язане з іменем, поштою чи рекламним ідентифікатором), статус покупки й базові технічні дані, які несе будь-який запит в інтернеті, як-от версію ОС та IP-адресу. Інших даних застосунок RevenueCat не надсилає. Див. політику RevenueCat, Apple і Google.

Збереження й завантаження поступу

У куточку батьків (за перевіркою для дорослих) «Зберегти поступ у файл» створює файл із поступом і відкриває системне меню «Поділитися», щоб ви зберегли його, де забажаєте. «Завантажити поступ із файлу» читає лише вибраний вами файл. Нам нічого не надсилається.
