Levain privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

Levain: Sourdough Baker is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It calculates sourdough formulas, reminds you to feed your starter, plans your bake with a reminder for every step and keeps a journal of your loaves. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • starters: names, feeding ratio and amount, where they are kept, feeding interval, and the feeding log with the times you fed them and the times they peaked;
  • bake plans: the steps, their durations and the time the bread should be ready, and your own schedule templates;
  • the calculator's current formula and the recipes you save;
  • journal entries: names, dates, ratings, fermentation times, notes, the formula used, and photos you take or pick. The app copies only the photos you choose into its own storage;
  • settings such as the weight unit and on-the-minute reminders.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

Reminders

Feeding and bake-step reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Their content (for example a starter's name or a step of your plan) is not sent to us or to any notification service.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Levain Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for feeding and bake-step reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android, optional): only if you turn on on-the-minute reminders in the app's settings.
  • Camera: only when you tap "Take a photo" for a journal entry.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are read.
  • Internet: only to verify purchases.

Ratings and support

If you rate the app, the dialog is shown by Apple or Google. If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and message and use them only to answer you.

Children

Levain is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Levain is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.
