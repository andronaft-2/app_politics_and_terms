SlabCalc privacy policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Operator: Dre Dev

EnglishEspañolУкраїнська

Summary

SlabCalc: Concrete & Rebar is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It calculates concrete, bags, rebar, mesh and forms. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run accounts or ads, and we don't sell or share your data. The app sends anonymous usage events so we can see which features help (see Usage analytics).

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • the last sizes you entered in each calculator;
  • your jobs: their names (for example a client name or address you type) and the pours and materials you add to them;
  • the prices you enter (ready-mix, bags, rebar, mesh, boards, stakes, fees);
  • settings such as units, currency, language and light or dark appearance, the day you first tried each Pro tool, and how many days you have opened the app;
  • the business name and phone you enter for order sheets.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete a job, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies.

Results and orders you send

When you tap the share button on a calculator or "Send order" on a job, the app opens your phone's share menu with the result as plain text (and a link to the app's store page). When you tap "PDF" on a job, the app builds an order sheet as a PDF on your phone (with your business name and phone, if you entered them) and opens the same share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it, the PDF is not kept by the app, and we never receive a copy.

Rating prompt

After a few days of use the app may ask once for a rating with the system dialog of the App Store or Google Play. The app does not see whether or how you rated it.

Purchases

SlabCalc Pro is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether you own Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase status, and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Usage analytics (PostHog)

In app versions released after 27 November 2026, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, Inc., a product analytics service; the data is stored in the EU (eu.i.posthog.com). The events show which screens and features are used, the onboarding steps, when the Pro screen is shown, when a purchase is started, completes or fails, when a PDF is exported, and when the app is installed, opened or moved to the background. Each event also carries the device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate country that PostHog works out from the IP address. IP addresses are not stored.

The events contain no names, email addresses or anything you type (sizes, job names, client names or addresses, prices or business details). There are no session recordings, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. The events are tied only to a random ID created by the app, not to who you are, and there is no account.

We use them to understand which features help and to fix the places where people get stuck on the way to a result or to Pro. The events are kept as long as needed for product analytics and then deleted. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: only to verify purchases and to send the usage events above.
  • The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos, and sends no notifications.

Children

SlabCalc is a tool for construction work and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

SlabCalc is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.

Resumen

SlabCalc: Concrete & Rebar es publicada por Dre Dev ("nosotros"). Calcula concreto, bolsas, varilla, malla y cimbra. Todo lo que escribes se queda en tu dispositivo. Datos guardados en tu dispositivo

La app guarda lo siguiente solo en su almacenamiento dentro de tu teléfono:

  • las últimas medidas que escribiste en cada calculadora;
  • tus obras: sus nombres (por ejemplo, el nombre o la dirección de un cliente que escribas) y los colados y materiales que les agregas;
  • los precios que escribes (premezclado, bolsas, varilla, malla, tablas, estacas, cargos);
  • ajustes como unidades, moneda, idioma y apariencia clara u oscura, el día en que probaste por primera vez cada herramienta Pro y cuántos días has abierto la app;
  • el nombre y el teléfono del negocio que escribes para los pedidos en PDF.

Estos datos no se envían a nosotros ni a nadie más. Se borran cuando eliminas una obra, borras los datos de la app o la desinstalas. Pueden incluirse en el respaldo de tu dispositivo (iCloud o Google), que se rige por las políticas de Apple y Google.

Resultados y pedidos que envías

Cuando tocas el botón de compartir en una calculadora o "Enviar pedido" en una obra, la app abre el menú de compartir de tu teléfono con el resultado como texto simple (y un enlace a la página de la app en la tienda). Cuando tocas "PDF" en una obra, la app crea en tu teléfono un pedido en PDF (con el nombre y el teléfono de tu negocio, si los escribiste) y abre el mismo menú de compartir. Nada se comparte hasta que eliges a dónde enviarlo, la app no guarda el PDF y nunca recibimos una copia.

Analítica de uso (PostHog)

En las versiones de la app publicadas después del 27 de noviembre de 2026, la app envía eventos de uso anónimos a PostHog, Inc., un servicio de analítica de producto; los datos se guardan en la UE (eu.i.posthog.com). Los eventos muestran qué pantallas y funciones se usan, los pasos de la introducción, cuándo se muestra la pantalla de Pro, cuándo una compra se inicia, se completa o falla, cuándo se exporta un PDF, y cuándo la app se instala, se abre o pasa a segundo plano. Cada evento incluye además el modelo del dispositivo, el sistema operativo y la versión de la app, y un país aproximado que PostHog deduce de la dirección IP. Las direcciones IP no se guardan.

Los eventos no contienen nombres, correos ni nada de lo que escribes (medidas, nombres de obras, nombres ni direcciones de clientes, precios ni datos del negocio). No hay grabaciones de sesiones, ni anuncios, ni rastreo en otras apps o sitios web. Los eventos solo están ligados a un identificador aleatorio que crea la app, no a quién eres, y no hay cuenta.

Los usamos para entender qué funciones ayudan y corregir los puntos donde la gente se atora camino a un resultado o a Pro. Los eventos se conservan el tiempo necesario para la analítica de producto y después se borran. Consulta la política de privacidad de PostHog.
