Dial In privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

EnglishУкраїнська

Summary

Dial In: Espresso & Pour-over is published by Dre Dev ("we"). It is a log for espresso shots and pour-over brews with a dial-in assistant and a brew timer. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • shots (dose, yield, time, grind setting, temperature, taste, rating and notes) and brews;
  • beans (name, roaster, origin, process, roast level and date, notes), grinders and your own recipes;
  • app settings such as the temperature unit.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete an entry, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is covered by Apple's and Google's policies. The dial-in suggestions are calculated on your device.

Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Dial In Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as OS version and IP address. See the RevenueCat privacy policy, the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Export and sharing

If you tap "Export CSV", the app creates a file on your device and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Permissions

  • Internet: only to verify purchases.
  • The screen stays on while the brew timer runs; this needs no permission and stops when the timer stops.

The app does not use your camera, photos, contacts, location, microphone or notifications.

Children

Dial In is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can view, change or delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Dial In is published by Dre Dev. Questions: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.
